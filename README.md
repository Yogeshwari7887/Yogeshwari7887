### 01 · AI Smart Traffic Management System

<table>
<tr><td>

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Completed-22C55E?style=flat-square&labelColor=0F172A"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Completed-January_2026-38BDF8?style=flat-square&labelColor=0F172A"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Category-Computer_Vision_%2F_IoT_Systems-38BDF8?style=flat-square&labelColor=0F172A"/>

</td></tr>
</table>

**Overview**
An intelligent traffic control system that uses real-time computer vision to detect emergency vehicles and dynamically create green corridors, reducing response times for ambulances and fire trucks.

**Problem Statement**
Emergency vehicles routinely get delayed at signal-controlled intersections because legacy traffic systems operate on fixed timers and cannot react to real-world conditions. Every minute of delay directly affects patient outcomes in medical emergencies.

**Solution**
The system uses a YOLOv8-based detection pipeline to identify emergency vehicles from live camera feeds, then communicates with a signal-control layer to clear a path through the intersection in real time, with a web-based control panel for monitoring and manual override.

### 03 · YourHearingEar — Personal Counseling Platform

<table>
<tr><td>

<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Completed-22C55E?style=flat-square&labelColor=0F172A"/>
<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Completed-September_2025-38BDF8?style=flat-square&labelColor=0F172A"/>

</td></tr>

**Overview**
A digital counseling platform designed to give users a safe, private space to seek support, built around ethical design and trust-first UX principles.

**Problem Statement**
Many people avoid seeking support because existing platforms feel impersonal or raise privacy concerns. There's a need for a respectful, low-friction digital space that prioritizes user trust from the first interaction.

**Solution**
The platform provides structured guidance modules and support frameworks with an interface designed around privacy, inclusivity, and trust-building — avoiding manipulative or attention-driven design patterns.
